# Music Concepts for Developers

1. **Stems:** Pre-separated, time-aligned audio tracks (Drums, Bass, Synth) exported at identical BPM.
2. **Low-Pass Filter (LPF):** Cutting higher frequencies ($300\text{Hz} \rightarrow 10,000\text{Hz}$) to change energy without altering tempo or pitch.
3. **Volume Ramping:** Automating parameter changes over $50\text{ms}–500\text{ms}$ intervals to eliminate digital pops/clicks.
